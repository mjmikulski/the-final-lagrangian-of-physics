# Method

The working contract for every report in this repo. Distilled from OpenWave's
`AI_HYGIENE.md`/`METHOD_NOTE.md` and substrate-framework's claim discipline;
deliberately minimal.

1. **Equations first, then code.** Every report states the exact objects it
   computes before showing results, and carries an equation-to-code map so a
   reader can check any number in minutes.

2. **Every number has a script.** No claim rests on prose. Committed results
   are regenerated by `reproduce.sh`, which asserts the structural content
   (counts, ranks, signs, identities) — the part that must survive a change of
   machine — and not the floating-point tails, which don't.

3. **Load-bearing claims get a second, independent route.** A different
   implementation, a different method, or an external replication — recorded
   in the report. AI-produced derivations are drafts until something that is
   not a language model confirms them.

4. **Claims carry explicit exclusions.** Each report says what it does *not*
   show, in its own section. Negative results and failed branches stay in the
   record; assumptions that belong to the model's author are flagged as
   author-gated rather than silently adopted.

5. **Provenance is pinned.** Upstream definitions are cited to exact files and
   commits, so "the current Lagrangian" always means one checkable object.

6. **Reports merge through review.** New reports land on a branch and open a
   pull request; an independent LLM critic reviews the PR (its review is
   posted as a PR comment), collaborators comment on GitHub, and only then
   is the report merged to `main`. Review findings are addressed in the PR
   or explicitly rebutted in its thread. (Reports 001–003 predate this
   rule and were merged directly.)

AI assistance (Claude) is used for derivation drafts, code, and text; a human
owns and can defend every published sentence.
